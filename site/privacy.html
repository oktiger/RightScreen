隐私政策

RightScreen · 生效日期 2026 年 10 月 13 日

RightScreen 不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人数据。

  • 应用不联网，没有账号、广告、统计或崩溃上报。
  • 应用通过 macOS 辅助功能权限读取窗口的位置、尺寸、标题和所属 App，仅用于在本机排列窗口和显示 Tab 标题；这些信息只在内存中使用，不会写入文件或离开你的 Mac。
  • 快捷键等偏好设置保存在本机的应用容器中，卸载应用即删除。

如本政策有变更，会更新本页面并修改生效日期。有疑问请联系 contact@zuijiao.com。

Privacy Policy (English)

RightScreen does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • The app makes no network connections and has no accounts, ads, analytics, or crash reporting.
  • With your Accessibility permission, the app reads window positions, sizes, titles, and owning apps solely to arrange windows and label tabs on your Mac. This information is kept in memory only and never leaves your device.
  • Preferences such as keyboard shortcuts are stored locally in the app's container and are removed when you delete the app.

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated. Contact: contact@zuijiao.com.
